COH Atlas Gameplay 0.11.0 — one AYN Thor session

Install this APK as an update over your existing COH character app. Keep app
storage, imported assets and the saved COHLOCAL / THORHERO. Tap Refresh runtime
once after updating, then Reopen saved THORHERO. Allow the normal startup.

1. Log in with COHLOCAL / offline, choose 127.0.0.1 and enter the existing
   THORHERO. Keep the current costume and powers.
2. After Saved character reopened, dismiss Welcome/help (choose None on the helper prompt), then tap
   Return to safe ground once. Stay still until Atlas position verified.
3. Check the Atlas plaza, statue, City Hall surfaces and your character. Take a
   screenshot of Atlas. The new walking controls become available here.
4. Use the left stick to walk forward/back and strafe left/right briefly.
   Release the stick and check that movement stops. Use X to jump once.
   The right stick remains the cursor; A clicks and B sends Escape. Hold a
   shoulder button with the right stick to try the game's camera look.
   The on-screen movement buttons provide the same walking and jump inputs.
5. Walk only a short distance for this first test. Check that you stay above
   the ground and scenery changes as you move. Stop all movement and wait
   60 seconds for fresh stationary server observations. Request Save within
   10 minutes of Return to safe ground.
6. Tap Save character / log out once. Keep controls neutral during the normal
   countdown. After Saved character verified, tap Finish and save report.
7. Export latest report and send the ZIP with the Atlas screenshot. Include
   whether scenery appeared, walking stopped when released, jump/camera worked,
   and the character stayed on the ground.

This test advances initial movement and Atlas materials. It adds 27 textures
requested by the City Hall/statue/plaza material definitions. Their appearance
needs this device observation. Wider collision,
combat, missions, audio and performance remain later steps. The app's existing
automated checks still verify preserved character state and ordinary saving;
visual world appearance and walking behavior require your observations.

If the scene remains empty, the character falls, or a step fails, tap Abort
operation, wait for cleanup and export the report. Preserve the character and
app data; a second long boot is not needed to report the failure.
