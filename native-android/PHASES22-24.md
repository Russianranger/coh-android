twelve authored directional clips remain active. Some four-wide bench collision
surfaces are inclined and source WALK can climb/follow them. The initial two-wide
blocking/sliding regression passes without changing the movement kernels.

New source/host checks pass: 14,842 map assertions, 4,000 metadata mutations,
byte-exact original hierarchy transforms, 20,736 matching render/collision float
components, 3,082 expanded WALK assertions, sanitized bridge/cache ownership and
independent original-decode/texture parity. The reusable real-GLES adapter check
passes on GLES 3.0 and 3.2, with 164,280 integrated transform components and zero
mismatches across three contexts. Repeated Near/Far changes and context recovery
retain three GEO/six texture decodes. These are source/host results; Android and
physical qualification are recorded separately in the evidence receipt.

Raw cache residency, bridge allocations and map metadata have separate bounded
counters. They do not measure total application RAM or sustained performance.
The small admitted corpus fits the cache; Near/Far proves selection changes,
while cache eviction/rollback is covered by the focused host tests. Original
raw inputs live in an explicit app envelope, not a claimed original PIGG file.
The material profile uses controlled neutral ambient/no fog and the original
grey fallback for the missing station detail texture. Full Atlas dependency
closure, environment lighting, broader model/material families, online login,
server replication and sustained Thor FPS remain unqualified.
